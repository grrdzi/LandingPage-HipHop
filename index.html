The Global Language of Hip-Hop

Born in the Bronx in the 1970s, hip-hop has evolved into a global cultural phenomenon that transcends borders, languages, and cultures. It's more than just music—it's a powerful form of expression that gives voice to the voiceless.

Today, hip-hop has been embraced and transformed by communities worldwide, each adding their unique cultural elements while staying true to the core pillars of the culture.

MCing/Rapping

The vocal delivery of rhymes and wordplay over beats.

DJing/Turntablism

The art of creating music with turntables and a mixer.

Graffiti Art

Visual expression through stylized writing and imagery.

Breaking/B-boying

Athletic street dance style with acrobatic movements.
